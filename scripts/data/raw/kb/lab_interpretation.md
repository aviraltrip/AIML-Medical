# Clinical Lab Interpretation Guidelines

## 1. Complete Blood Count (CBC)
- **Hemoglobin (Hb):** 
    - Normal: 13.5-17.5 g/dL (Male), 12.0-15.5 g/dL (Female).
    - Low: Anemia (Check for fatigue, pallor).
- **White Blood Cell (WBC):**
    - Normal: 4,500-11,000 cells/mcL.
    - High: Leukocytosis (Sign of infection, inflammation, or stress).
    - Low: Leukopenia (Immunosuppression).
- **Platelets:**
    - Normal: 150,000-450,000/mcL.
    - Low: Thrombocytopenia (Risk of bleeding/bruising).

## 2. Metabolic Panels
- **Glucose (Fasting):**
    - Normal: 70-99 mg/dL.
    - High: Hyperglycemia (Check for Diabetes).
- **Creatinine:**
    - Normal: 0.7-1.3 mg/dL.
    - High: Potential kidney dysfunction.
- **Potassium (K+):**
    - Normal: 3.6-5.2 mmol/L.
    - High/Low: Dangerous for heart rhythm.

## 3. Cardiac Markers
- **Troponin (I or T):**
    - High: Indicates heart muscle damage (Myocardial Infarction).

## 4. Inflammatory Markers
- **CRP (C-Reactive Protein):**
    - High: General indicator of inflammation or infection.
- **D-Dimer:**
    - High: Potential blood clot (PE or DVT).
