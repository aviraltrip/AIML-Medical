# Clinical Triage Guidelines (Synthesis of WHO ETAT & ESI)

## 1. Emergency Severity Index (ESI) - 5-Level Triage
- **Level 1: Resuscitation (Immediate)**
    - Patient is dying. Requires immediate life-saving intervention.
    - Examples: Cardiac arrest, massive trauma, severe respiratory distress, coma.
- **Level 2: Emergent (Within 10-15 mins)**
    - High-risk situation, confused/disoriented, or in severe pain/distress.
    - Examples: Chest pain (possible MI), stroke symptoms, severe allergic reaction.
- **Level 3: Urgent (Stable, multiple resources needed)**
    - Vitals are stable but require multiple diagnostic tests or medications.
    - Examples: Abdominal pain, stable fractures.
- **Level 4: Less Urgent (Stable, one resource needed)**
    - Examples: Simple laceration, sore throat with fever.
- **Level 5: Non-Urgent (No resources needed)**
    - Examples: Prescription refills, minor rashes.

## 2. WHO ETAT (Pediatric Specific)
### Emergency Signs (Treat Immediately):
- **A (Airway):** Obstructed breathing.
- **B (Breathing):** Severe respiratory distress, central cyanosis.
- **C (Circulation):** Cold hands, capillary refill > 3s, weak/fast pulse (Shock).
- **C (Coma/Convulsions):** Unconscious or actively seizing.
- **D (Dehydration):** Lethargy, sunken eyes, skin pinch goes back very slowly in a child with diarrhea.

### Priority Signs (Assess Quickly):
- Tiny baby (< 2 months).
- High fever.
- Severe pain.
- Respiratory distress (not severe).
- Urgent referral from another facility.

## 3. Vital Sign "Red Flags" (Adult)
- **Heart Rate:** > 120 bpm or < 50 bpm.
- **Systolic BP:** < 90 mmHg or > 180 mmHg.
- **SpO2:** < 92% on room air (or < 88% for COPD patients).
- **Respiratory Rate:** > 25 breaths/min or < 10 breaths/min.
- **Temperature:** > 39.0°C (102.2°F) or < 35.0°C (95.0°F).
