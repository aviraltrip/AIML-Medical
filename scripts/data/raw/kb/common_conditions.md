# Common Medical Conditions & Diagnostic Pointers (NHS Grounded)

## 1. Cardiovascular
### Myocardial Infarction (Heart Attack)
- **Symptoms:** Central chest pain (heavy, pressure, or tight band), radiation to left arm, jaw, neck, back, or stomach. Associated with sweating, lightheadedness, and shortness of breath.
- **Red Flag:** Pain not relieved by rest or nitroglycerin.

### Stable Angina
- **Symptoms:** Chest pain triggered by physical exertion or stress.
- **Key Pointer:** Relieved by rest or medication within a few minutes.

## 2. Respiratory
### Pneumonia
- **Symptoms:** Productive cough (yellow/green sputum), fever, chills, chest pain that worsens with deep breathing (pleuritic pain), shortness of breath.
- **Key Pointer:** Crackles heard on lung auscultation.

### Pulmonary Embolism (PE)
- **Symptoms:** Sudden onset shortness of breath, sharp chest pain (worse with breathing), coughing up blood (hemoptysis).
- **Risk Factors:** Recent long-haul flight, surgery, or leg swelling (DVT).

## 3. Gastrointestinal
### Appendicitis
- **Symptoms:** Pain starting near the navel (periumbilical) moving to the Right Lower Quadrant (RLQ). Fever, nausea, vomiting, loss of appetite.
- **Key Pointer:** Pain increases with movement, coughing, or vibration (McBurney's point tenderness).

### Cholecystitis (Gallstones)
- **Symptoms:** Severe pain in the Right Upper Quadrant (RUQ) or epigastrium, often radiating to the right shoulder blade. Often occurs after fatty meals.

## 4. Neurological
### Stroke (FAST)
- **Face:** Drooping on one side.
- **Arms:** Inability to lift both arms.
- **Speech:** Slurred or garbled.
- **Time:** Critical to seek immediate care.

### Meningitis
- **Symptoms:** High fever, severe headache, stiff neck (nuchal rigidity), photophobia (light sensitivity), non-blanching rash (purple spots).
